1. What this site collects

This site collects only two interactions: a like on a project, and a first name that a visitor chooses to type after pressing the follow button. The hosting provider may also process standard technical data, such as an IP address and a browser type, in its server logs.

2. How the name is used

The first name is stored only so the owner can acknowledge the follow request. No account, password, email address or profile is created, and no other personal data is requested.

3. How likes are used

A like is recorded against a project and is aggregated into a single total. Likes are not linked to a name and are not shown to visitors individually.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies, uses no tracking pixels, and does not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Local browser storage is used only to remember interactions on this device, such as a liked project or a follow already submitted.

5. Where the data is stored

The like count and the submitted names are stored on Supabase, which acts as a data processor for this site. Contact with the owner happens outside this site, through email, WhatsApp, phone or social platforms, and those services apply their own privacy policies.

6. Retention

Submitted names are kept indefinitely unless deletion is requested.

7. Deletion requests

A visitor can ask for their name to be deleted at any time through the email or WhatsApp link in the contact section. The request is handled manually and no other action is required.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The last updated date is shown at the top of this section.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
